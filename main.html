AI Interview Chatbot

A mock job interview app where an AI interviewer acts as an HR executive. Users enter their name, experience and skills, choose a seniority level, job position and target company, then answer five live, streamed interview questions. At the end, the app scores the candidate from 1 to 10 and gives written feedback.

Tech Stack:

  • Python - Streamlit
  • Groq API (openai/gpt-oss-120b LLM)
  • python-dotenv - streamlit-js-eval

Main Objective:

  • Help candidates practice realistic job interviews with an LLM
  • Give an objective score and actionable feedback

Deployment:

  • Streamlit Community Cloud
  • API key managed through a .env file

Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)

A comparative deep learning study that recognizes 7 emotions from speech on the TESS dataset (2,800 audio files). MFCC features were used to train CNN, Simple RNN and Vision Transformer (ViT) models, reaching ~99.8% accuracy with CNN, 97.4% with ViT and 86.9% with RNN. Extended with fine-tuning of transformer audio models (Wav2Vec2 and emotion2vec+).

Tech Stack:

  • TensorFlow / Keras - PyTorch
  • Hugging Face Transformers (Wav2Vec2, emotion2vec+)
  • Librosa (MFCC) - Scikit-learn - Seaborn

Main Objective:

  • Compare CNN, RNN and ViT architectures for emotion recognition
  • Evaluate fine-tuned pre-trained audio transformers

Deployment:

  • Jupyter Notebooks (GPU training)
  • Research paper and presentation

Multi-Task WiFi CSI Sensing (MSc Thesis)

Master's thesis on sensing human activity through walls using WiFi Channel State Information (CSI). A deep learning model performs multi-task detection of multiple activities across different users, environments and devices, trained on the CSI-Bench dataset and evaluated cross-dataset on WiMANS, XRF55 and Widar.

Tech Stack:

  • Python - PyTorch (Deep Learning)
  • CSI-Bench - WiMANS - XRF55 - Widar datasets
  • ESP32 boards with external antennas

Main Objective:

  • Detect multiple activities across users, environments and devices
  • Support smart-building monitoring and emergency response

Deployment:

  • In progress (expected June 2027)
  • Real-world CSI data collection with ESP32
